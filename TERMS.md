# Terms

Author: Michael Tyler, M.Ed.  
Date: April 2026

## Governed Multi-Agent Intelligence System (GMAIS)

A structured approach to coordinating AI systems through governance, role separation, context boundaries, continuity discipline, and human approval controls.

## Relationally-Governed Intelligence System (RGIS)

A framework for describing how relational context, identity continuity, trust, authority, and human oversight shape AI system behavior across time.

## V9 Governance

A governance posture in which execution is treated as a validated outcome rather than an automatic default.

## Continuity-Oriented Memory

A design principle in which memory is treated as a structural component of continuity and coherence, not merely a temporary cache.

## Human Approval Boundary

A control point where human authorization is required before state-changing, reputation-impacting, financial, public, or sensitive actions are taken.
