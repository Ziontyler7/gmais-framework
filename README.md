# GMAIS Framework

**Governed Multi-Agent Intelligence System**  
Author: **Michael Tyler, M.Ed.**  
Initial public authorship record: **April 2026**

## Purpose

This repository is a public authorship and citation anchor for Michael Tyler's work on governed multi-agent intelligence, relational governance, continuity-oriented AI systems, and human-centered AI architecture.

It is intended to make the work easier to find, evaluate, cite, and connect across public sources.

## Core Terms

### GMAIS

**GMAIS** stands for **Governed Multi-Agent Intelligence System**.

In public-safe terms, GMAIS describes a structured approach to coordinating AI systems through governance, role separation, context boundaries, continuity discipline, and human approval controls.

### RGIS

**RGIS** stands for **Relationally-Governed Intelligence System**.

In public-safe terms, RGIS describes the relational layer of governed AI behavior: how identity, continuity, trust, authority, and human oversight shape system behavior over time.

### V9 Governance

**V9** refers to a governance model associated with validation before execution, continuity preservation, and constraint-aware system behavior.

## Why This Matters

As AI systems become more capable, the central challenge is no longer raw output alone. The harder problem is how to preserve continuity, governance, accountability, trust, and human authority across extended AI workflows.

GMAIS is one public framework for discussing that problem.
